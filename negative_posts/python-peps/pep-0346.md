This PEP is a combination of :pep:'s "Reliable Acquisition/Release
Pairs" with the "Anonymous Block Statements" of Guido's :pep:. This
PEP aims to take the good parts of :pep:, blend them with parts of
:pep: and rearrange the lot into an elegant whole. It borrows from
various other PEPs in order to paint a complete picture, and is
intended to stand on its own.

During the discussion of :pep:, I maintained drafts of this PEP as
PEP 3XX on my own website (since I didn't have CVS access to update a
submitted PEP fast enough to track the activity on python-dev).

Since the first draft of this PEP, Guido wrote :pep: as a simplified
version of :pep:. :pep: (at the time of writing) uses the exact
same semantics for the new statements as this PEP, but uses a slightly
different mechanism to allow generators to be used to write statement
templates. However, Guido has indicated that he intends to accept a
new PEP being written by Raymond Hettinger that will integrate :pep:
and :pep:, and will permit a generator decorator like the one
described in this PEP to be used to write statement templates for PEP
The other difference was the choice of keyword ('with' versus
'do') and Guido has stated he will organise a vote on that in the
context of :pep:.

Accordingly, the version of this PEP submitted for archiving on
python.org is to be WITHDRAWN immediately after submission.
