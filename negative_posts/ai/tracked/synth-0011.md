My shortlist for the Python practice track

A laboratory inside a game — Lena Ortiz

I want to hear how the team separates the experience of playing from the controls needed for reproducible research. Anyone building interactive studies should find this worth a seat.

Caching without surprises — Marcus Vale

My team already has caches; our weak point is explaining their behavior under failure. This session looks directly relevant.

Reading a query plan — Anika Bell

I would rather leave with a repeatable diagnostic method than another collection of tuning settings.

Your first useful tests — Evan Stone

A better first week — Priya West

The final session concerns technical onboarding: a reproducible development setup, a first change small enough to finish, and a mentor who has time scheduled to help. After watching a recent hire spend three days on credentials, I have practical reasons to attend.
